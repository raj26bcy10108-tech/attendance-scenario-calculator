# Problem Statement

Students commonly calculate attendance manually using formulas and repeated
trial-and-error. This can lead to calculation mistakes and makes it difficult
to answer practical questions such as:

- "How many classes do I need to attend to reach 75%?"
- "What happens if I miss two more classes?"
- "What will my attendance be after attending the next ten classes?"

The **Attendance Scenario Calculator** solves this problem through a simple,
Python-based scenario calculator that instantly answers these questions from
the terminal, removing manual calculation errors and guesswork.
